## Summary — top 3 gaps visafact.org should close

1. **No per-product pricing context.** All four competitors display monthly cost ranges for the products they cover. Visafact.org's checker produces a compliance status (GREEN / RED / UNKNOWN) but provides no price signal to help a reader prioritize. A pricing-context note on visa pages — even a range pulled from product pages at snapshot date — would reduce the gap without compromising the evidence-based standard. No price claim should be published without a dated source.

2. **No practical in-country context section.** Nomadwise.io pages devote substantial space to "what it is like in Spain / Portugal" — upfront payment norms in private clinics, regional healthcare differences, sports coverage nuances. Visafact.org's visa pages are compliance-focused and do not address practical friction points that affect the same reader choosing between visa routes. Adding a standardized "practical considerations" section (sourced to authority guidance where possible, or explicitly marked as informational) would fill this gap across all visa pages.

3. **Spain-specific carrier comparison absent.** Healthinsuranceforspanishvisas.com names DKV, Sanitas, ASISA, Adeslas with from-prices and explains why each works for the DNV. Visafact.org maps these carriers but does not expose them in a human-readable carrier table on the visa page. A "products that passed for this route" table — derived from the existing mapping data — would make the compliance checker results immediately actionable without requiring a reader to run the checker themselves.
