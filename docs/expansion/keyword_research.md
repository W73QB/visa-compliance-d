## Priority classification rationale

**Priority A** — Clear mandatory insurance requirement documented in multiple authority-level sources; specific coverage attributes stated (coverage amount, authorized insurer, no-deductible rule); products in the existing repo plausibly GREEN.

**Priority B** — Insurance requirement is ambiguous or partial (Indonesia: sources disagree; Netherlands: business liability vs health insurance distinction; Mexico: consulate-by-consulate variation). Building a data model for these routes requires direct authority source verification before a visa page can be published.

**Priority C** — Insurance is explicitly NOT a mandatory visa requirement based on the authority source reviewed. A content page is not justified unless the angle changes to practical insurance recommendation rather than compliance checking (which falls outside visafact.org's stated purpose).
