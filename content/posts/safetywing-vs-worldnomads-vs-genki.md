## Short answer

This comparison uses evidence-backed product facts and route-specific visa requirements. Spain DNV requires comprehensive, unlimited health coverage with no deductibles, co-payments, or moratorium, and requires insurers authorized to operate in Spain (Source: `BLS_ES_DNV_LONDON_2026`, locator: page 2, item 9; verified 2026-01-12). The evidence for SafetyWing, World Nomads Explorer, and Genki Traveler conflicts with those requirements, so all three are RED for Spain DNV in the current snapshot.

## What the authority requires

- Insurance is mandatory for all applicants. (Source: `BLS_ES_DNV_LONDON_2026`, locator: page 2, item 9; verified 2026-01-12)
- Insurer must be authorized to operate in Spain. (Source: `BLS_ES_DNV_LONDON_2026`, locator: page 2, item 9; verified 2026-01-12)
- Policy must cover risks insured by Spain's public health system. (Source: `BLS_ES_DNV_LONDON_2026`, locator: page 2, item 9; verified 2026-01-12)
- Coverage must be comprehensive, full, and unlimited. (Source: `BLS_ES_DNV_LONDON_2026`, locator: page 2, item 9; verified 2026-01-12)
- No deductible, no co-payments, and no moratorium are allowed. (Source: `BLS_ES_DNV_LONDON_2026`, locator: page 2, item 9; verified 2026-01-12)

## FAQ

**Q: Can any of these three products pass Spain DNV?**
**A:** No. Spain DNV requires unlimited, Spain-authorized health coverage with no deductibles or co-payments (Source: `BLS_ES_DNV_LONDON_2026`, locator: page 2, item 9; verified 2026-01-12), and the evidence for these products shows deductibles or coverage limits (Sources: `SAFETYWING_WEBSITE_2026`, `WORLDNOMADS_COMPARE_2026`, `GENKI_TRAVELER_COVERAGE_2026`).

**Q: Why are SafetyWing and World Nomads RED for Germany freelance?**
**A:** Germany states that travel insurance is not sufficient for national D visas (Source: `DE_D_VISA_HEALTH_INSURANCE_2026`, locator: Health insurance requirements for national (category D) visas; verified 2026-01-15). SafetyWing is labeled travel insurance in its evidence summary, and World Nomads is presented as travel insurance on its comparison page (Sources: `SAFETYWING_WEBSITE_2026`, `WORLDNOMADS_COMPARE_2026`).

**Q: Why does Genki show UNKNOWN for Germany freelance?**
**A:** Germany requires health insurance commensurate with statutory coverage (Source: `DE_D_VISA_HEALTH_INSURANCE_2026`). Genki evidence lists deductible, coverage cap, and a maximum insurance period, but does not explicitly confirm statutory-level coverage or Germany-specific acceptance, so the checker does not infer compliance (Source: `GENKI_TRAVELER_COVERAGE_2026`).

**Q: Why are all three GREEN for Portugal DNV?**
**A:** The current Portugal E11 checklist evidence only encodes mandatory insurance (Source: `VFS_PT_E11_CHINA_2025`, locator: page 1, General Requirements; verified 2026-01-15), which all three products satisfy in the snapshot.
