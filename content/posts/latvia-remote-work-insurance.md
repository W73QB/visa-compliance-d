## Short answer

Latvia's long-term remote work visa requires a health insurance policy valid in the Republic of Latvia and in the Schengen Member States, with a minimum insurance liability limit of at least EUR 42,600 during the insurance period (Source: `LV_REMOTEWORK_PMLP_2026`, Office of Citizenship and Migration Affairs (PMLP); verified 2026-06-13). The engine models three rules: insurance is mandatory, minimum coverage of 42,600 EUR, and the policy must be valid in Latvia.

## What the authority requires

- A health insurance policy is a required document. (Source: `LV_REMOTEWORK_PMLP_2026`; verified 2026-06-13)
- The policy must be valid in the Republic of Latvia and in the Schengen Member States. (Source: `LV_REMOTEWORK_PMLP_2026`; verified 2026-06-13)
- The minimum insurance liability limit may not be less than EUR 42,600 during the insurance period. (Source: `LV_REMOTEWORK_PMLP_2026`; verified 2026-06-13)
