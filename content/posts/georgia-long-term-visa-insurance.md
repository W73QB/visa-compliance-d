## Short answer

A Georgia long-term visa requires travel/health insurance valid for the period of stay in Georgia, valid all over Georgia, and (for a long-term visa) valid for the validity period of the visa (Source: `GE_LONGVISA_GEOCONSUL_2026`, Ministry of Foreign Affairs of Georgia, Consular Department; verified 2026-06-13). The published criteria state no coverage amount, so the engine models three rules: insurance is mandatory, valid in Georgia, and covering the visa validity period.

## What the authority requires

- The alien shall hold travel/health insurance for the period of stay in Georgia. (Source: `GE_LONGVISA_GEOCONSUL_2026`; verified 2026-06-13)
- The travel/health insurance shall be valid all over Georgia. (Source: `GE_LONGVISA_GEOCONSUL_2026`; verified 2026-06-13)
- For a long-term visa, the insurance must be valid for the validity period of the visa. (Source: `GE_LONGVISA_GEOCONSUL_2026`; verified 2026-06-13)
- The published criteria state no coverage amount, so that stays UNKNOWN.
