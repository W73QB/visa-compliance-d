## Short answer

All non-EEA students are required to have private medical insurance when coming to and residing in Ireland for the purpose of study (Source: `IE_STUDENT_ISD_2026`, Immigration Service Delivery; verified 2026-06-13). Where travel insurance suffices, it must cover the student for one full year (or the entirety of a shorter stay) with a minimum of EUR 25,000 for accident and EUR 25,000 for disease, plus any period of hospitalisation. The engine models three rules: insurance is mandatory, minimum coverage of 25,000 EUR, and full-period cover.

## What the authority requires

- All non-EEA students must have private medical insurance for study in Ireland. (Source: `IE_STUDENT_ISD_2026`; verified 2026-06-13)
- The insurance should cover accident and/or disease and any period of hospitalisation. (Source: `IE_STUDENT_ISD_2026`; verified 2026-06-13)
- Where travel insurance suffices: it must cover the student for one full year (or the entirety of a shorter stay), with a minimum of 25,000 EUR for accident and 25,000 EUR for disease. (Source: `IE_STUDENT_ISD_2026`; verified 2026-06-13)

## How we evaluate

The checker compares each modeled requirement against product evidence. Three rules are encoded from the ISD page: insurance is mandatory, the documented coverage limit must be at least 25,000 EUR (currency-aware), and the policy must cover one full year or the entirety of a shorter stay. A monthly subscription that can lapse does not assure full-period cover, so it is YELLOW; a full-period policy meeting the threshold is GREEN; a product whose documents state no overall limit stays UNKNOWN.

An important limitation, disclosed rather than modeled: Immigration Service Delivery accepts travel insurance only at first registration when it meets these thresholds; at second and subsequent registrations travel insurance is not accepted and a fuller private medical policy is required. Confirm your registration stage before relying on a travel policy. This follows the UNKNOWN > Wrong principle. See /methodology/ for the full logic.

## Proof package checklist

- Private medical insurance covering accident, disease and any period of hospitalisation.
- A documented limit of at least 25,000 EUR for accident and for disease.
- Cover for one full year (or the entirety of a shorter stay).
- For second and later registrations, a full private medical policy rather than travel insurance.

## FAQ

**Q: What is required?**
**A:** Private medical insurance; where travel insurance suffices, one full year of cover with a minimum of 25,000 EUR for accident and disease (Source: `IE_STUDENT_ISD_2026`; verified 2026-06-13).

**Q: Does travel insurance count?**
**A:** Only at first registration if it meets the thresholds; not at later registrations.

**Q: Why is a monthly subscription YELLOW?**
**A:** It can lapse before the year ends, so it does not assure full-period cover; a full-period policy meeting the 25,000 EUR minimum is GREEN.
