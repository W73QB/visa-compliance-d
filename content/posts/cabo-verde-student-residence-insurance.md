## Short answer

A Cabo Verde residence authorisation for a higher-education student requires the applicant to have health insurance (seguro de saude), per immigration law Lei n. 66/VIII/2014, art. 53 (Source: `CV_STUDENTRES_DGTCV_2026`, Boletim Oficial da Republica de Cabo Verde; verified 2026-06-16). The law states no coverage amount or territory, so the engine models a single rule: insurance is mandatory.
