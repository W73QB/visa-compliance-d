## Short answer

A Bulgaria long-stay Visa D requires an insurance policy for the whole period of stay, issued by an insurance company licensed in the European Union, with a minimum amount of coverage of 30,000 euros, and the policy must cover repatriation costs as well as emergency medical care and emergency hospital treatment (Source: `BG_LONGSTAY_MFA_2026`, Ministry of Foreign Affairs of the Republic of Bulgaria, Visa D (long-term) document; verified 2026-06-17).

## What the authority requires

The Ministry of Foreign Affairs Visa D (long-term) document lists, among the documents required in all cases:

- An "Insurance policy (and copy) for the whole period of stay, issued by an Insurance Company with a license to carry out insurance activities on the territory of the European Union, with a minimum amount of coverage of 30,000 euros." (Source: `BG_LONGSTAY_MFA_2026`; verified 2026-06-17)
- "The policy should cover all repatriation costs as well as emergency medical care and emergency hospital treatment for the period of stay."

## How we evaluate

The checker compares each modeled requirement against product evidence. Four rules are encoded from item 5 of the Visa D document: insurance is mandatory, a minimum of 30,000 euros, validity for the whole period of stay, and an insurer licensed in the European Union. The repatriation, emergency medical care and emergency hospital treatment wording is preserved in the evidence excerpt.

The EU-licence condition is the deciding one. Following the UNKNOWN > Wrong principle, a product is only credited for it when its own evidence documents that the insurer holds an EU insurance licence; otherwise the engine records UNKNOWN rather than assuming any provider qualifies. As a result, AXA Schengen Europe Travel is GREEN for this route, because its source documents an insurer (Inter Partner Assistance SA/NV) authorized by the National Bank of Belgium, with 100,000 euros of medical cover, hospitalization and medical repatriation. The other tracked products do not document an EU insurance licence in their evidence, so they stay UNKNOWN — including products with high limits and full-period cover, because the licence itself is not established. ## Proof package checklist

- An insurance policy for the whole period of stay from an EU-licensed insurer.
- Coverage of at least 30,000 euros, covering repatriation, emergency medical care and emergency hospital treatment.
- A certificate showing the policy holder, policy number and coverage dates.
- The remaining Visa D documents (passport, accommodation, financial means, police clearance).

## FAQ

**Q: What is required?**
**A:** An EU-licensed insurance policy of at least 30,000 euros, for the whole period of stay, covering repatriation and emergency hospital treatment (Source: `BG_LONGSTAY_MFA_2026`; verified 2026-06-17).

**Q: Which product is GREEN?**

**Q: Is the 30,000 euro the Schengen short-stay figure?**
