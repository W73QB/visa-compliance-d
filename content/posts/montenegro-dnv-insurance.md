## Short answer

Montenegro's digital nomad temporary residence permit requires the applicant to attach proof that they have health insurance (Source: `ME_DNV_GOVME_2026`, Government of Montenegro digital nomads portal; verified 2026-06-13). The official page states no coverage amount, period or territory, so the engine models a single rule: insurance is mandatory.
