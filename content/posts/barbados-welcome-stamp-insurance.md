## Short answer

The 12 Month Barbados Welcome Stamp requires that the applicant be the holder of valid health insurance for the period for which the Stamp was granted (Source: `BB_WELCOMESTAMP_OAG_2026`, Remote Employment Act 2020-23, Section 3(c); verified 2026-06-13). The Act states no coverage amount or territory, so the engine models two rules: insurance is mandatory, and it must cover the full Stamp period.

## What the authority requires

- The applicant must be the holder of valid health insurance. (Source: `BB_WELCOMESTAMP_OAG_2026`; verified 2026-06-13)
- The insurance must cover the period for which the Stamp was granted (a 12-month Stamp). (Source: `BB_WELCOMESTAMP_OAG_2026`; verified 2026-06-13)
- The Act states no coverage amount and no territory, so those stay UNKNOWN.

## How we evaluate

The checker compares each modeled requirement against product evidence. Two rules are encoded from the Remote Employment Act: insurance is mandatory, and it must cover the period for which the Stamp was granted (the Stamp is granted for 12 months). A monthly subscription that can lapse does not assure full-period coverage, so it is YELLOW; a policy covering the whole period is GREEN.
