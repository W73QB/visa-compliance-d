## Short answer

New Zealand's Fee Paying Student Visa requires, as a condition of the visa, that you have insurance for travel and any health care you need, from the start of your course until your visa expires (Source: `NZ_STUDENT_INZ_2026`, Immigration New Zealand; verified 2026-06-13). Immigration New Zealand states your education provider will tell you what the policy must cover and sets no fixed amount. The engine models two rules: insurance is mandatory, and it must cover the full visa period.

## What the authority requires

- Insurance for travel and health care is a condition of the student visa. (Source: `NZ_STUDENT_INZ_2026`; verified 2026-06-13)
- The cover must run from the start of your course until your visa expires. (Source: `NZ_STUDENT_INZ_2026`; verified 2026-06-13)
- Your education provider determines what the policy must cover; INZ states no fixed amount. (Source: `NZ_STUDENT_INZ_2026`)
