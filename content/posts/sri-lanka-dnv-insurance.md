## Short answer

Sri Lanka's Digital Nomad Visa requires international health insurance that covers medical care in Sri Lanka, listed among the documents all applicants must submit (Source: `LK_DNV_IMMIGRATION_2026`, Department of Immigration and Emigration; verified 2026-06-13). The official document states no coverage amount, so the engine models two rules: insurance is mandatory, and the policy must cover medical care in Sri Lanka.
