## Short answer

Sweden's residence permit for higher-education studies of less than one year requires comprehensive health insurance, valid for the entire time you will be in Sweden, covering costs for emergency and other medical care (Source: `SE_STUDY_MIGVERKET_2026`, Swedish Migration Agency; verified 2026-06-13). The engine models three rules: insurance is mandatory, it must be comprehensive, and it must cover the full stay.

## What the authority requires

- Comprehensive health insurance is required for studies that will last less than a year. (Source: `SE_STUDY_MIGVERKET_2026`; verified 2026-06-13)
- The insurance must be valid for the entire time you will be in Sweden. (Source: `SE_STUDY_MIGVERKET_2026`; verified 2026-06-13)
- It must cover costs for emergency and other medical care. (Source: `SE_STUDY_MIGVERKET_2026`; verified 2026-06-13)
