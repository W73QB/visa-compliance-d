## Short answer

An Anguilla student permit requires health insurance (for the applicant and any dependents), inclusive of coverage commencement and end dates, among the documents to attach to the application (Source: `AI_STUDENT_GOVAI_2026`, Government of Anguilla Higher Education Licensing Board; verified 2026-06-16). The process states no coverage amount or territory, so the engine models a single rule: insurance is mandatory.
