## Short answer

Albania's Unique Permit (Leje Unike) for the digital mover (lëvizës digjital) category requires a health-insurance policy valid for at least one year, for the permit period (Source: `AL_LEJE_UNIKE_MB_2026`, official Leje Unike Digital Mover checklist; legal basis Law 79/2021 art. 68 and VKM 858 of 29.12.2021; verified 2026-06-09). The official checklist states the one-year validity but no coverage amount, so the checker models that insurance is mandatory and must cover the full permit period, and leaves any figure UNKNOWN.

## What the authority requires

- A health-insurance policy is mandatory for the unique permit. (Source: `AL_LEJE_UNIKE_MB_2026`; verified 2026-06-09)
- The policy must be valid for at least one year (the permit period). (Source: `AL_LEJE_UNIKE_MB_2026`; verified 2026-06-09)
- The official checklist states no coverage amount and no insurer-authorization rule, so those stay UNKNOWN.

## How we evaluate

The checker compares each modeled requirement against product evidence. Two rules are encoded from the official Ministry of Interior checklist (legal basis Law 79/2021 and VKM 858/2021): insurance is mandatory, and the policy must be valid for at least a year. A monthly subscription that can be cancelled mid-stay does not assure cover for the full permit period, so it is YELLOW; a policy covering the whole period is GREEN.

A note on the 30,000 EUR figure that circulates for this permit: it is a third-party "route-hardening" suggestion, not the baseline requirement quoted in the official Ministry of Interior checklist, so the engine does not encode a coverage amount. This follows the UNKNOWN > Wrong principle: only what an official source states is modeled. See /methodology/ for the full logic.

## FAQ

**Q: What insurance is required?**
**A:** A health-insurance policy valid for at least one year (Source: `AL_LEJE_UNIKE_MB_2026`; verified 2026-06-09).

**Q: Is 30,000 EUR an official requirement?**
**A:** No. The official checklist states only one-year validity; the 30,000 EUR figure is a third-party hardening suggestion, not the baseline, so it is not encoded.

**Q: Why is a monthly subscription YELLOW?**
**A:** It can lapse before the year ends, so it does not assure full-period cover; a full-period policy is GREEN.
