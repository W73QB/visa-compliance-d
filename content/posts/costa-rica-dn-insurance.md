Costa Rica's Executive Decree 43619 requires health insurance for digital nomads with at least US $50,000 in medical coverage, and the policy must cover the entire authorized legal stay period (Source: `CR_DECREE_43619_2026`, locator: Article 9; verified 2026-01-12).
