## Short answer

Croatia's temporary stay for digital nomads requires proof of health insurance for the period of time you plan to be in Croatia, and the travel or private health insurance must cover the territory of the Republic of Croatia (Source: `HR_MUP_DIGITALNOMAD_2026`, Required documents; verified 2026-06-10). The checker models three rules from this: insurance is mandatory, it must cover the full period, and it must cover Croatian territory. No coverage amount is stated, so that stays UNKNOWN.

## What the authority requires

- Health insurance is mandatory for the stay. (Source: `HR_MUP_DIGITALNOMAD_2026`, Required documents; verified 2026-06-10)
- The insurance must be valid for the whole period of the intended stay in Croatia. (Source: `HR_MUP_DIGITALNOMAD_2026`; verified 2026-06-10)
- The travel or private health insurance must cover the territory of the Republic of Croatia. (Source: `HR_MUP_DIGITALNOMAD_2026`; verified 2026-06-10)

## Proof package checklist

- A policy whose certificate states coverage of the territory of Croatia (not only a home country or a vague region).
- Validity for the full period you plan to be in Croatia, not a month-to-month subscription that can lapse.
- Documented medical cover for the stay.

## Common rejection traps

- A domestic health policy that does not document coverage in Croatia.
- A monthly subscription that can be cancelled before the stay ends.
- Insurance whose territorial scope is unclear about Croatia.
