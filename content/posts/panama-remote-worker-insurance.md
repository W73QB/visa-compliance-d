## Short answer

Panama's Short-Stay Visa as Remote Worker (Visa de Corta Estancia como Trabajador Remoto) requires a copy of the applicant's medical insurance policy that must maintain coverage in the national territory and stay valid for the applicant's period of stay (Source: `PA_REMOTEWORK_SNM_2026`, Servicio Nacional de Migracion; verified 2026-06-13). The engine models three rules: insurance is mandatory, the policy must cover Panamanian territory, and it must stay valid for the period of stay.

## What the authority requires

- A copy of the applicant's medical insurance policy is a required document. (Source: `PA_REMOTEWORK_SNM_2026`; verified 2026-06-13)
- The policy must maintain coverage in the national territory. (Source: `PA_REMOTEWORK_SNM_2026`; verified 2026-06-13)
- The policy must stay valid for the applicant's period of stay. (Source: `PA_REMOTEWORK_SNM_2026`; verified 2026-06-13)
- The official requirements state no minimum coverage amount.
