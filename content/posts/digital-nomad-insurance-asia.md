Asia routes vary widely. Some list explicit coverage requirements, while others omit insurance entirely. VisaFact marks those cases as NOT_REQUIRED or UNKNOWN depending on evidence.

If a route does not list insurance, that is **not** the same as “insurance never requested.” Some applicants are asked for proof after submission. Keep a policy summary ready, and confirm the latest checklist for your authority before applying.
