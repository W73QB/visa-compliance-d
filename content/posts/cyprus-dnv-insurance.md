## Short answer

The Cyprus Digital Nomad Visa requires a certificate of health insurance for medical care that covers inpatient and outpatient care and transportation of corpse, in the Plan A category (Source: `CY_MD_DIGITALNOMAD_DOCS_2026`, List of Accompanying Documents, item 7; verified 2026-06-09). The official document states the coverage scope but no amount, so the checker models that insurance is mandatory, comprehensive, and valid for care in Cyprus, and leaves any coverage figure UNKNOWN.

## What the authority requires

- A certificate of health insurance is mandatory for the residence permit. (Source: `CY_MD_DIGITALNOMAD_DOCS_2026`, item 7; verified 2026-06-09)
- The insurance must cover inpatient and outpatient care and transportation of corpse. (Source: `CY_MD_DIGITALNOMAD_DOCS_2026`, item 7; verified 2026-06-09)
- The cover must be the Plan A category, i.e. health insurance valid for medical care in Cyprus. (Source: `CY_MD_DIGITALNOMAD_DOCS_2026`, item 7; verified 2026-06-09)

## Common rejection traps

- Submitting a domestic policy that does not document coverage for care in Cyprus.
- Relying on the secondary-source 30,000 EUR figure: the official list states the coverage scope, not an amount, so match the scope (inpatient, outpatient, repatriation).
- A certificate that omits one of the listed coverages.
