## Short answer

Luxembourg's third-country pupil/student authorisation to stay (over three months) requires a health insurance certificate (Luxembourgish or foreign) covering all risks on Luxembourg territory (Source: `LU_STUDENT_GUICHET_2026`, Guichet.lu / Directorate of Immigration; verified 2026-06-14). The page states no coverage amount, so the engine models three rules: insurance is mandatory, it must cover Luxembourg, and it must be comprehensive.

## What the authority requires

- A health insurance certificate is a required supporting document. (Source: `LU_STUDENT_GUICHET_2026`; verified 2026-06-14)
- It must cover all risks on Luxembourg territory (a Luxembourgish or foreign certificate is accepted). (Source: `LU_STUDENT_GUICHET_2026`; verified 2026-06-14)
- The page states no coverage amount, so that stays UNKNOWN.
