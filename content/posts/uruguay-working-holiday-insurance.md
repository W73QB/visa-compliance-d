## Short answer

Uruguay's Working Holiday for foreign citizens requires the applicant to have comprehensive health insurance (un seguro de salud integral) (Source: `UY_WORKINGHOLIDAY_GUBUY_2026`, Government of Uruguay / Direccion de Migracion Internacional; verified 2026-06-14). The page states no amount or territory, so the engine models two rules: insurance is mandatory, and it must be comprehensive.

## What the authority requires

- A health insurance (seguro medico) is a required item. (Source: `UY_WORKINGHOLIDAY_GUBUY_2026`; verified 2026-06-14)
- It must be comprehensive (seguro de salud integral). (Source: `UY_WORKINGHOLIDAY_GUBUY_2026`; verified 2026-06-14)
- The page states no amount and no territory, so those stay UNKNOWN.
