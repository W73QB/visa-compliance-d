## Short answer

Slovenia's Long-Stay Visa (Type D) requires travel medical insurance for the period of the validity of the visa, valid in the entire Schengen area, with minimum coverage of 30,000 EUR, covering emergency medical expenses, hospital treatment and repatriation (Source: `SI_D_GOVSI_2026`, Ministry of Foreign Affairs of the Republic of Slovenia, Long Stay Visa D checklist; verified 2026-06-13). The engine models three rules: insurance is mandatory, minimum coverage of 30,000 EUR, and full-period coverage.

## What the authority requires

- Travel medical insurance is a required document for all Long-Stay (D) applicants. (Source: `SI_D_GOVSI_2026`; verified 2026-06-13)
- It must cover the period of the validity of the visa. (Source: `SI_D_GOVSI_2026`; verified 2026-06-13)
- Minimum coverage of 30,000 EUR, valid in the entire Schengen area, covering emergency medical expenses, hospital treatment and repatriation. (Source: `SI_D_GOVSI_2026`; verified 2026-06-13)

## How we evaluate

The checker compares each modeled requirement against product evidence. Three rules are encoded from the MFA Long Stay Visa D checklist: insurance is mandatory, the documented coverage limit must be at least 30,000 EUR (currency-aware), and the policy must cover the visa validity period. A monthly subscription that can lapse does not assure full-period coverage, so it is YELLOW; a full-period policy meeting the threshold is GREEN; a product whose documents state no overall limit stays UNKNOWN.

This is the long-stay national visa's own requirement (legal basis: the Foreigners Act), not the Schengen short-stay rule, even though the figure is the same and the cover must be valid Schengen-wide. See /methodology/ for the full logic.

## FAQ

**Q: How much coverage is required?**
**A:** At least 30,000 EUR, for the visa validity period (Source: `SI_D_GOVSI_2026`; verified 2026-06-13).

**Q: Is this the Schengen rule?**
**A:** No. It is Slovenia's own Long-Stay (D) requirement under the Foreigners Act; the figure matches but the route differs.

**Q: Why is a monthly subscription YELLOW?**
**A:** It can lapse before the visa expires, so it does not assure full-period coverage; a full-period policy meeting the 30,000 EUR minimum is GREEN.
