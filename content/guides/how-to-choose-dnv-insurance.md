## What the authority requires

Different routes have different rules. Examples from current evidence:
- Spain DNV requires comprehensive, unlimited health insurance with no deductibles or co-payments (source_id: `BLS_ES_DNV_LONDON_2026`).
- Germany national D visas state that travel insurance is not sufficient (source_id: `DE_D_VISA_HEALTH_INSURANCE_2026`).
- Thailand DTV does not list insurance as a requirement (source_id: `TH_MFA_DTV_2026`).

## Step-by-step selection flow

1. **Identify your exact route.** Use the official checklist for your consulate or authority, not a generic summary.
2. **Match the requirement type.** If travel insurance is rejected, you need documented health insurance coverage instead.
3. **Confirm coverage constraints.** Some routes require unlimited coverage, no deductibles, or full-period payment. Only select products that prove those terms in evidence.
4. **Run the checker with a snapshot.** This ensures the evaluation uses the same evidence you reviewed.

If the authority requires authorization in a specific country (for example, Spain), ask the insurer for a certificate showing local authorization, not just global marketing language. Keep a copy of that certificate with your application packet so the requirement is visible in one page.
