## Common questions

**What insurance does the Albania Unique Permit (Digital Mover) require?**  
The official checklist requires a health-insurance policy valid for at least 1 year for the permit period.

**Is a 30,000 EUR coverage amount an official requirement?**  
No. The official Ministry of Interior checklist states only that the policy must be valid for at least 1 year; the 30,000 EUR figure is a third-party route-hardening suggestion, not the baseline requirement, so the engine does not encode it.

**Why is a monthly subscription marked YELLOW?**  
The policy must be valid for at least a year. A month-to-month policy that can lapse does not assure full-period coverage, so it is YELLOW; a full-period policy is GREEN.
