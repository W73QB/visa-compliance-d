## Short answer

Germany's Federal Foreign Office states that applicants for national (category D) visas must have health insurance commensurate with the minimum level of German statutory health insurance, and that travel insurance is not sufficient for any D visa application (Source: `DE_D_VISA_HEALTH_INSURANCE_2026`, locator: Health insurance requirements for national (category D) visas; verified 2026-01-15).

This requirement is stricter than typical travel insurance. Even high coverage limits do not substitute for statutory-level health coverage. The certificate must clearly indicate that the policy is health insurance and that it meets the statutory minimum standard described by the authority.

- Health insurance policy or certificate showing statutory-level coverage.
- Documentation that the policy is health insurance (not travel insurance).
- If the policy is not in German, include a summary that explicitly references statutory coverage equivalence.
- If the insurer provides a statutory-equivalence statement, include it alongside the certificate to reduce follow-up requests.
