## Short answer

Portugal E11 (VFS China route) requires valid travel insurance that covers necessary medical expenses, urgent medical assistance, and possible repatriation (Source: `VFS_PT_E11_CHINA_2025`, locator: page 1, General Requirements; verified 2026-01-15). The checklist does not specify a minimum coverage amount in this route.

Because this route only lists coverage types, the safest approach is to submit a certificate that explicitly names those three items. If your policy uses different wording, ask the insurer for an addendum or a summary that mirrors the checklist language to avoid ambiguity.
