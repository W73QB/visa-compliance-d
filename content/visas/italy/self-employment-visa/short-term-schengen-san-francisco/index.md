## Common questions

**How much medical coverage does the Italy short-term self-employment (Schengen) visa require?**  
The Consulate General of Italy states the Schengen minimum: proof of 30,000 euro (or 50,000 US dollars) in medical expenses coverage for the duration of the trip.

**Is travel insurance accepted for this route?**  
Yes. The consulate notice lists travel medical insurers (including World Nomads) as acceptable, as long as the medical expenses coverage meets the minimum.

**Does trip cancellation or repatriation count toward the minimum?**  
No. The consulate states that non-medical services such as trip cancellation, lost baggage, or repatriation of remains do not count toward the 30,000 euro medical-expenses requirement.
