## Common questions

**What insurance does the Sri Lanka Digital Nomad Visa require?**  
The Department of Immigration and Emigration requires International Health Insurance that covers medical care in Sri Lanka, among the documents all applicants must submit.

**Why is only Genki Native GREEN for this route?**  
The policy must cover medical care in Sri Lanka. Genki Native documents global coverage that includes Sri Lanka; the other products do not document Sri Lanka validity, so the engine records UNKNOWN rather than assuming a foreign policy qualifies.

**Is there a minimum coverage amount?**  
The official document states no coverage amount, so the engine encodes none and models that insurance is mandatory and must cover medical care in Sri Lanka.
