## FAQ

**Q: Is insurance required for Thailand DTV?**
**A:** No. The official DTV requirements list does not include insurance (Source: `TH_MFA_DTV_2026`, verified 2026-01-12).

**Q: What documents are required instead?**
**A:** Passport bio page, recent photo, proof of location, financial proof (500,000 THB), and employment contract or portfolio (Source: `TH_MFA_DTV_2026`, verified 2026-01-12).

**Q: What does NOT_REQUIRED mean?**
**A:** It means the official list does not require insurance; it does not mean insurance is discouraged.

**Q: Can the requirement change?**
**A:** Yes. Always compare the evidence date to your application date and re-check the official list.
