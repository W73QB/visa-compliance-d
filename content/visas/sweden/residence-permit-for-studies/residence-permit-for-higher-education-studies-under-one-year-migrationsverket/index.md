## Common questions

**What insurance does a Sweden study residence permit (under one year) require?**  
The Swedish Migration Agency requires comprehensive health insurance for studies under one year, valid for the entire time in Sweden, covering emergency and other medical care.

**Why are some products UNKNOWN?**  
The cover must be comprehensive. Products whose documents do not establish comprehensive coverage (the travel-medical products) stay UNKNOWN, while products documenting comprehensive cover are GREEN.

**Does it need to last the whole stay?**  
Yes. The insurance must be valid for the entire time you will be in Sweden, so a full-period policy is required.
