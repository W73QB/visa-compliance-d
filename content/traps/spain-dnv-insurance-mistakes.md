## Short answer

Spain DNV insurance mistakes usually come from missing explicit proof for the checklist requirements: Spain authorization, unlimited coverage, and no deductible/co-payment/moratorium. The BLS London checklist lists these requirements on page 2, item 9 (Source: `BLS_ES_DNV_LONDON_2026`, verified 2026-01-12).

## What the authority requires

- Insurer authorized to operate in Spain. (Source: `BLS_ES_DNV_LONDON_2026`, locator: page 2, item 9; verified 2026-01-12)
- Comprehensive, full, unlimited coverage. (Source: `BLS_ES_DNV_LONDON_2026`, locator: page 2, item 9; verified 2026-01-12)
- No excess (deductible) or co-payments, and no moratorium. (Source: `BLS_ES_DNV_LONDON_2026`, locator: page 2, item 9; verified 2026-01-12)
- Coverage of risks insured by Spain's public health system. (Source: `BLS_ES_DNV_LONDON_2026`, locator: page 2, item 9; verified 2026-01-12)

## How we evaluate

The checker compares product evidence against each requirement above. If evidence explicitly contradicts a requirement, the result is RED. If evidence is missing, the result is UNKNOWN rather than inferred. See /methodology/ for rule logic and the UNKNOWN > Wrong principle.

## Proof package checklist

- Policy certificate showing unlimited coverage with no deductibles or co-payments.
- Documentation confirming insurer authorization to operate in Spain.
- Evidence that the policy covers risks insured by Spain's public health system.

## Common rejection traps

- Policies with coverage caps (Spain requires unlimited coverage).
- Deductibles or co-payments listed in policy documents.
- Certificates that omit Spain authorization or public system risk coverage.
- INFERENCE: Relying on marketing summaries without explicit policy wording leads to UNKNOWN outcomes.

## FAQ

**Q: What is the most common rejection reason?**
**A:** Missing explicit proof for Spain authorization, unlimited coverage, and zero deductible/co-payments, which are required in the checklist (Source: `BLS_ES_DNV_LONDON_2026`, page 2, item 9; verified 2026-01-12).

**Q: Are deductibles or co-payments allowed?**
**A:** No. The checklist requires no excess or co-payments and no moratorium (Source: `BLS_ES_DNV_LONDON_2026`, page 2, item 9; verified 2026-01-12).

**Q: Does the insurer have to be authorized in Spain?**
**A:** Yes. The checklist requires insurance contracted with an insurer authorized to operate in Spain (Source: `BLS_ES_DNV_LONDON_2026`, page 2, item 9; verified 2026-01-12).

**Q: Why do some products show UNKNOWN for Spain DNV?**
**A:** UNKNOWN means the product evidence does not explicitly confirm one or more requirements. The checker does not infer compliance without explicit proof.
