Genki Native: Your Comprehensive International Health Insurance

Genki Native is an international health insurance designed for long-term global living, offering flexible and complete coverage with no maximum time limit.

🕒 Duration & Cancellation

  • No maximum duration — lifetime coverage possible.

  • Minimum insurance period: 12 months.

  • After 12 months, you can cancel monthly.

🌍 Global Coverage

  • All countries are covered — including those with travel warnings.

  • You can stay abroad as long as you want — no return-to-home requirement.

💳 Payment

  • Premiums are paid monthly via credit or debit card (different payment methods also available)

🧾 Plan Options: Basic vs. Premium

Genki Native comes in two variants so you can choose your level of benefits:

Genki Native Basic

  • €1,000,000 maximum coverage per insurance year

Genki Native Premium

  • No overall maximum coverage limit

  • More extensive benefits (see below)

✅ Shared Benefits (Both Plans)

  • Accidents & emergencies

  • Outpatient and inpatient medical care

  • Direct payment to hospitals for inpatient care

  • Worldwide free choice of doctors and hospitals

  • Emergency and medically necessary transport

  • Prescribed medication, treatments, and aids

  • Physiotherapy (if prescribed)

  • Palliative care

  • Organ transplants

  • Telemedicine

🏥 Inpatient Care Includes

  • Accommodation & meals

  • Medically necessary treatments, surgeries, and devices

  • Psychiatric inpatient care

  • Second medical opinion (with prior approval)

🔝 Additional Benefits with Genki Native Premium

  • Single-room hospital accommodation

  • Home nursing care after hospitalization

  • Preventive care (checkups, vaccinations, etc.) — up to €250/year

  • Alternative care (e.g. osteopathy, chiropractic) — up to €500/year

  • Dental care — up to €2,000/year (cleanings up to €200/year)

  • Vision care (glasses, contacts) — up to €250/year

  • Maternity care (after 12-month waiting period)

  • Inpatient & outpatient psychotherapy (prior approval needed)

❌ Not Covered

  • Professional sports (earning compensation)

  • Active participation in war

  • Travel issues (trip cancellation, delays, baggage loss, etc.)

  • Lifestyle & cosmetic products (e.g. hair growth, vitamins, cosmetics)

  • Contraceptives, tonics, and non-medical wellness

💰 Deductibles

You can reduce your premium by choosing a deductible:

  • €1,000 / year

  • €500 / year

  • Deductibles only apply to outpatient care

  • Inpatient care is always covered in full without deductible

⏳ Waiting Periods

  • 12 months for maternity, major dental (inlays, crowns, bridges), and dentures

  • Preventive & alternative care: covered under Premium only, with limits

  • Vision, psychotherapy, and dental: Premium only

  • Emergency maternity complications are covered without waiting period

  • Cross-border treatment: covered with prior approval
