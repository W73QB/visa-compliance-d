What’s covered?

Full medical coverage

It includes emergencies, accidents, consultations, diagnostic tests, surgeries (both inpatient and outpatient), and hospitalizations.

Medication

If an unforeseen illness requires you to take medication prescribed by a doctor, this is covered. Without copayments or deductibles.

Unlimited doctor access

You will go to any doctor that accepts private/self-paying patients, pay upfront, and then submit all invoices/receipts through your Feather account.

EU-wide coverage

Expat health insurance covers you in Spain and for temporary trips to the countries of the European Union, the Schengen countries, Andorra, Monaco, San Marino and the Vatican City.

Dental treatment

If you need to see a dentist, pain relief treatments are covered up to €500/year. Tooth replacement after an accident is also covered up to €500.

Repatriation

In particularly serious cases, your insurance covers your return to your home country for treatment or in case of fatality, for burial.

What isn’t covered?

Pre-existing conditions

Treatments related to pre-existing conditions are not covered.

Routine check-ups

Medical visits without any symptoms or complaints and any conditions discovered during those are not covered.

Regular vaccinations

Expat Insurance does not cover routine or travel-related vaccinations.

Mental health therapy

If you need to get psychotherapy appointments or treatments, those cannot be covered by the policy.
