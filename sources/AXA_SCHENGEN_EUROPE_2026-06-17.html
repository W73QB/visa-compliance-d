FAQ about AXA Essential Insurance for Europe

Are you leaving for Europe soon and wondering whether travel insurance is really necessary? We'll clarify everything for you in simple terms and answer all your questions about the Essential plan, helping you choose the protection best suited for your next European adventure.

Do I really need travel insurance for a trip to Europe?

If you need a Schengen visa, then yes. Schengen travel insurance is mandatory for being granted one. In other words, no insurance = no visa.

However, even if you don't need a Schengen visa, do you really want to risk paying unexpected medical bills, losing your belongings or documents, or facing emergency repatriation alone? A small accident abroad can turn your dream trip into a costly nightmare.

With the right insurance plan, you get 24/7 support and peace of mind, knowing you and your family are protected if anything goes wrong. It's always better to be safe than sorry.

Did you know?

A medical repatriation in Europe typically costs between €5,000 and €20,000. If a medical plane is needed, the bill can rise significantly, depending on the severity of the situation and the distance to be covered.

How much does AXA ESSENTIAL travel insurance cost?

Starting at as low as €8.90, AXA's ESSENTIAL takes care of both you and your loved ones throughout the entire duration of your trip. And that includes all the 'what ifs' — big or small — during your stay in Europe.

Want even more security? You can customize your insurance with additional options.

In which countries am I covered by AXA ESSENTIAL insurance?

Just like the Schengen area itself, AXA's ESSENTIAL plan's coverage is (almost) borderless! In other words, a single subscription is enough to be protected on most of the European continent (and often well beyond).

List of countries covered:

  • Schengen countries + EU countries: Austria, Belgium, Bulgaria, Croatia, Cyprus, Czech Republic, Denmark, Estonia, Finland, France, Germany, Greece, Hungary, Italy, Iceland, Ireland, Liechtenstein, Latvia, Lithuania, Luxembourg, Malta, Netherlands, Norway, Poland, Portugal, Romania, Slovakia, Slovenia, Spain, Sweden, and Switzerland
  • European microstates: Andorra, Monaco, San Marino, and the Vatican
  • United Kingdom: England, Northern Ireland, Scotland, and Wales

Is AXA ESSENTIAL insurance valid for a Schengen visa?

Yes. All of AXA's Schengen travel insurance including AXA ESSENTIAL, meet the European Union's requirements for short-stay (up to 90 days) and long-stay visas.

Once you subscribe, you'll instantly receive the AXA Travel Insurance Certificate by email, which you can download and print anywhere. This certificate is accepted by all Schengen embassies, consulates, and visa centers. Just attach it to your visa application.

REMINDER: If your visa is refused, AXA usually offers a full refund when you provide an official refusal letter from the relevant authority. AXA is rooting for you!

What to do if I get sick during my trip to Europe?

If you fall ill during your trip to Europe, local authorities will provide first aid. Your first step is to dial 112, the emergency number across Europe. Rest assured, AXA is also available 24/7 with a team of medical assistance experts ready to support you wherever you are.

In an emergency, contact AXA's call center as soon as possible using the number on your insurance certificate. Remember to provide your policy number, which starts with "SCH." You can also use the Doctor Please! teleconsultation service with the same credentials included on your certificate.

PRO-TIPS:

- Always keep the numbers above saved both on paper and your phone, along with the emergency numbers for the European region you're in.

- Make sure to share this info with your travel companions or a trusted contact who can be reached easily if needed.
